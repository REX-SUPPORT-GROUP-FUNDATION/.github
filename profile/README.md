# Rex Support Group Foundation

We are a Polish nonprofit foundation (Fundacja Rex Support Group, Lublin) working on research and development, technology for business and accessibility projects.

## Our project: AI Wallet

**Your company data in one wallet for AI.** Mail, ERP, invoices and messengers connected to Claude, ChatGPT and Cursor, with a two-year memory and built-in personal data protection.
